Privacy Policy

UXV Mobile, UXV Theater and UXV Desktop · Universal eXperience Ventures LLC · Effective 26 November 2026

UXV Mobile (iPhone and iPad), UXV Theater (Apple TV) and UXV Desktop (Mac) show your own music, photos, videos, contacts and places in one simple carousel. This policy explains what the apps use and where it goes. The short version: we do not collect your data. There are no UXV accounts, no UXV servers, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your device

When you allow it, the apps read your music library, photos, contacts, location and (UXV Mobile only) motion activity. They use them on your device to show your content, and do not send them to us. You can change each permission at any time in Settings on your device.

  • Music, photos and videos are read from your libraries and played or shown on the device.
  • Contacts are shown in People. Copying your address book to iCloud, so your other UXV devices can show it, is off until you turn it on.
  • Location is used for local weather, nearby places and directions.
  • Motion activity (UXV Mobile) is used only to notice that you are in a moving vehicle, so the app can ask once per trip whether you are a passenger.
  • Settings, favorites, audiobook bookmarks and picture caches are stored on the device. Picture caches are cleared each time the app starts.

Your iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, the apps keep your settings and your People and Travel lists in your own private iCloud account, so all your UXV devices show the same thing. This data is stored by Apple under your Apple Account. We cannot see it. You can remove it from your iCloud settings at any time.

Services the apps contact

To show live content, the apps request it directly from these services. Each request carries only what is needed to answer it (for example a place name, or your approximate location for weather) and your device's IP address, as any internet request does. Each service's own privacy policy applies.

  • Apple: Apple Maps and MapKit (places, maps, directions), Apple Weather, Apple Music and iCloud.
  • OpenWeather: weather, if Apple Weather is unavailable.
  • Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons: photos of places and works of art.
  • Museum collections: The Metropolitan Museum of Art, Art Institute of Chicago, Cleveland Museum of Art, National Gallery of Art, Rijksmuseum, SMK – National Gallery of Denmark, the Smithsonian Institution and NASA.
  • Radio stations: the station you choose streams to you from its own servers.
  • LibriVox and the Internet Archive: free public-domain audiobooks.
  • Google (YouTube) and Dropbox: only if you connect them; see below.

Directions can open in Apple Maps, and calls open in Phone or FaceTime. Those apps are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Accounts you connect (YouTube and Dropbox)

In Settings › Accounts you can connect your own YouTube (Google) account and your own Dropbox account. Connecting is optional. You sign in on Google's or Dropbox's own page, and the apps never see your password. We have no servers, so nothing from these accounts is ever sent to us.

YouTube

The apps use YouTube API Services. With your permission (read-only, “View your YouTube account”), the app reads your subscriptions, playlists and liked videos, with their titles and thumbnails, to list them in Video › Streaming. Videos play in YouTube's own player, and on Apple TV in the YouTube app.

  • The app keeps your sign-in token in your device's Keychain and caches the lists and thumbnails on the device, refreshing or deleting them within 30 days.
  • It cannot upload, change, like, comment on or delete anything in your account. It never uses your data for advertising, and never sells or shares it.
  • To disconnect: Settings › Accounts › YouTube › Disconnect revokes the token and deletes everything the app stored from your device at once. Deleting data in the app does not change anything stored by YouTube itself.
  • To revoke access separately: at any time, on Google's security settings page, security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • By using the YouTube features, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's use of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • UXV's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Dropbox

With your permission (read-only), the app reads your folder names, file names and the photos and videos you open, to show them in Photo › Cloud. It also reads your account name, to show which account is connected.

  • Your token is kept in the Keychain and thumbnails are cached on the device.
  • The app cannot add, change, move, share or delete files.
  • To disconnect: Settings › Accounts › Dropbox › Disconnect, or remove UXV under Connected apps in your Dropbox account settings. The Dropbox Privacy Policy applies to Dropbox.

Network shares

If you add a file server on your home network, such as a NAS or a computer sharing a folder (Settings › Network), the app asks for Local Network access. It stores the server's address, user name and password in your device's Keychain, and reads folders and files straight from your server over your home network. Removing the server deletes those details.

Beta testing

If you test a beta version through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares with us the information described in TestFlight & Privacy: your name and email address as you were invited, crash reports, and any feedback and screenshots you choose to send. We use these only to fix and improve the apps, and do not share or sell them.

Children

The apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version starts collecting anything (for example anonymous usage counts), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
